The trap

The race to the trap

Yes, as long as black stays in its double corners. From every quiet position with black in both double corners and white to move, white forces a trap in both phases, against every shuttling defence, in at most 12 moves. The game computed: black only shuttles, and white makes quiet moves that leave black no capture; a shuttle move that gives white a capture costs black a king at once. The longest defence: black shuttles as long as possible, white builds the trap as fast as possible and forces the exchange after the last shuttle move.

Black can also leave a corner itself. According to the database that is often just as good as shuttling, and usually black can later get back into the corner, as the black king at the bottom right does in the main line at black in the double corners (moves 10 to 13). It saves nothing: every position stays lost.
