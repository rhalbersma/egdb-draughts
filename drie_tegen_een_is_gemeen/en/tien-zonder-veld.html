Without h8: the zugzwang

White on h10, j10 and i9, black on j8, white to move: D. i9 and j10 are locked in, every move of h10 allows a double capture (1. h10-g9 j8×h10×f8), and 1-1 is drawn. All moves: 1. h10-g9 D, 1. h10-f8 D, 1. h10-e7 D, 1. h10-d6 D, 1. h10-c5 D, 1. h10-b4 D, 1. h10-a3 D.
