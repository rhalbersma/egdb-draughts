Endgame databases for draughts

Exact endgame databases for international draughts and for checkers, on boards of any size: every position is computed to the end of the game, with the distance in plies. Three sites replay classic analyses and a fourth collects the solution of 6×6, with clickable moves and for every position the value and all moves.

6×6 Is Strongly SolvedDraughts on the 6×6 board is strongly solved: every position up to 12 pieces, with the exact distance to the end. The start position is a draw; the opening tree to 4 plies, the longest win per number of pieces and the history of the solution. Strategie der 36 veldenHerm Jan Brascamp's series on the 6×6 board (2016), checked chapter by chapter with exact databases. Drie tegen een is gemeenTwo and three kings against one on boards of any size: the win on 6×5, the families, the trapping positions and 10×10 with one square removed. Checkers: 3 kings against 2Short kings on boards of any size: blockades, the double-corner fortress and opposition.

The programs, databases and reports: github.com/rhalbersma/egdb-draughts. The form of the analyses is taken from those by Wieger Wesselink on 10x10.org.
