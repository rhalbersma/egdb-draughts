Traps on 8×8

3 against 2, exception 2: two curiosities

Two of the 316 drawn classes of 3 against 2 on 8×8 fit none of the families of the blockades: only one white king is locked in the single corner, and still white makes no progress.

One white king is locked in the corner: after 4-8 black captures 12×3. Against the black king on 10 the two other white kings make no progress, and 11-8 and 11-16 even lose. All moves of white: 1. 2-7 D, 1. 2-6 D, 1. 4-8 D, 1. 11-7 D, 1. 11-15 D, 1. 11-8 L50, 1. 11-16 L34.

The black kings stand in white's single corner, on 3 and 8, in front of the white king on 4. It cannot move: 8 is occupied, and it cannot capture because 11 is occupied. Only 11-7 keeps the draw; the other moves lose. All moves of white: 1. 11-7 D, 1. 11-16 L10, 1. 15-10 L4, 1. 15-19 L4, 1. 15-18 L4.
