Endgame databases for draughts

Exact endgame databases for international draughts and for checkers, on boards of any size: every position is computed to the end of the game, with the distance in plies. Three sites replay classic analyses, with clickable moves and for every position the value and all moves.

Strategie der 36 veldenHerm Jan Brascamp's series on the 6×6 board (2016), checked chapter by chapter with exact databases up to 10 pieces; plus the solution of the start position: a draw. Drie tegen een is gemeenTwo and three kings against one on boards of any size: the win on 5×6, the families, the trapping positions and 10×10 with one square removed. Checkers: 3 kings against 2Short kings on boards of any size: blockades, the double-corner fortress and opposition.

The programs, databases and reports: github.com/rhalbersma/egdb-draughts.
