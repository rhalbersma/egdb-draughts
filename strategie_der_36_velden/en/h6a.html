Click a move to see the position on the large board; ‹ › (or the arrow keys) step through the line, ⏮︎ ⏭︎ jump to its start and end, ▶︎ plays it. Values are for the side to move; for a move, for the side making it, with n the number of plies to the end of the game, the move included: W = win (odd), L = loss (even), D = draw. Blue = main line, green = side line, italics = continuation by the database, ? = a move that changes the result according to the database, with the better move or the refutation in [ ] (≡ = back to the position before that move), * = the only move that keeps the win or the draw. The # button turns the square names on the large board on or off; the button with the range of the square names (e.g. 1…18 or a1…f6 on 6×6) switches between numeric and algebraic notation, in the text as well.
