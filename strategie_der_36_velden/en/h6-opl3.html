Blue = main line, green = side line, italics = continuation by the database, ? = a move that changes the result according to the database, with the better move or the refutation in [ ] (≡ = back to the position before that move), * = the only move that keeps the win or the draw. The # button turns the square names on the large board on or off; the button with the range of the square names (e.g. 1…18 or a1…f6 on 6×6) switches between numeric and algebraic notation, in the text as well.
