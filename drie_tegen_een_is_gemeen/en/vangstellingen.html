Trapping positions

Each family has a trapping position for the three white kings that is fixed relative to a corner or to the V. Kept at the same place on every board size, the conversion always takes exactly the same number of plies. The strong side builds the trapping position from an arbitrary position and then converts quickly: on 9×17 the longest win is 23 plies, of which at most 8 are the conversion.

On 10×11 (diagram 8 of the book) white to move wins within 5 plies on every square of the black king, except h10, the hatched square in the book, which takes 9 plies. The king at b2 stands on the long diagonal, whose other end is an entrance at the top right; the two kings at the top right form a half trap with it and the upper one can close the long diagonal.

The V (only for H = 2W − 1): the diagonals from the two corners of one long side meet exactly in the middle of the opposite side. Shorter, and the legs cross below the edge; longer, and they hit the edge at two different squares. Even W always has the two corners; odd W has them only with colouring p1.
