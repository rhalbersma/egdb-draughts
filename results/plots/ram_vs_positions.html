RAM against the positions whose groups fit

Share of all positions whose smallest group (one configuration of men with all its king placements; kings-only materials divided by their 4-fold symmetry) fits in a group budget of 75 % of the machine's RAM. Feasible: 1 bit per position, one side to move (rung 3). Comfortable: rungs 0–2, at 2 bits for both sides (WDL) or a depth byte and 4 bit planes for both sides (DTM, MTC). The last step of every curve is the all-kings material.
